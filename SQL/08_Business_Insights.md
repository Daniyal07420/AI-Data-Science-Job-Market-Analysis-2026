# Phase 8 - Business Insights & Recommendations

## Project
Global AI & Data Science Jobs Analysis 2026

---

## Executive Summary

This report summarizes the key findings from SQL analysis performed on the AI Jobs dataset.

---

## Business Insights

### 1. Experience vs Salary

- Senior professionals earn significantly higher salaries.
- Salary increases with years of experience.

**Recommendation:**
Companies should retain experienced employees through competitive compensation.

---

### 2. Top Paying Job Roles

- AI Architect
- Machine Learning Engineer
- AI Research Scientist

**Recommendation:**
Professionals should develop advanced AI skills to maximize earning potential.

---

### 3. Company Size

- Large companies generally pay higher salaries.
- Medium companies offer competitive salaries.
- Small companies may compensate with equity and career growth.

---

### 4. Remote Work

Remote jobs continue to offer competitive salaries for skilled AI professionals.

**Recommendation:**
Organizations should maintain flexible remote work policies.

---

### 5. Certifications

Employees with more certifications generally earn higher salaries.

**Recommendation:**
Organizations should sponsor professional certifications.

---

### 6. AI Tool Usage

Daily AI tool users receive higher average salaries.

**Recommendation:**
Encourage AI tool adoption and provide internal training.

---

### 7. Education

Higher education levels are associated with better salaries.

---

### 8. Geographic Analysis

Salary varies across countries based on demand and market conditions.

---

### 9. Job Satisfaction

Higher salaries often correspond with better job satisfaction.

---

### 10. Final Conclusion

The analysis shows that experience, AI skills, certifications, education, and continuous learning are the strongest factors affecting salary growth.

Organizations investing in employee development and AI adoption are more likely to attract and retain top talent.
